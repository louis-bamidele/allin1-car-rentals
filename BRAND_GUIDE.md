# All in 1 Car Rentals — Brand Guide

A practical brand reference used to guide the website build. Designed to keep visuals, voice, and content consistent across pages.

---

## 1. Brand Snapshot

- **Company name:** All in 1 Car Rentals
- **Tagline:** Your Key to a Rental Car
- **Promise:** Reliable, well kept vehicles and friendly local service for every kind of trip.
- **Personality:** Trustworthy, warm, professional, easygoing. We speak like a helpful local, not a corporate desk agent.

## 2. Logo

The primary mark is a wordmark with a stylized car silhouette swoosh above. The "1" doubles as a car key, reinforcing the tagline.

- **Primary logo:** Full color on white or light backgrounds.
- **Reverse logo:** Use a light or white version on deep navy and dark photography.
- **Clear space:** Keep padding around the logo equal to the height of the "A" in "ALL".
- **Minimum width:** 120 px on screen.
- **Do not:** Recolor, stretch, add drop shadows, place on busy photography without an overlay, or rotate.

## 3. Color System

Pulled from the logo. Navy carries authority, gold adds warmth and a premium feel.

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Navy 900 | `#0F1B4C` | Primary brand color, headings, buttons, navbar background |
| Navy 700 | `#1E2D6B` | Secondary surfaces, hover states |
| Navy 500 | `#2E4191` | Accents, links |
| Gold 500 | `#D8A24A` | Primary accent, CTA highlights, dividers |
| Gold 400 | `#E6B968` | Hover for gold buttons, light accents |
| Cream 50 | `#FAF6EE` | Section background, soft contrast |
| Slate 700 | `#334155` | Body text |
| Slate 500 | `#64748B` | Muted text, captions |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Default background |

Pairings to use:
- Navy 900 background with Gold 500 accents and white text.
- White background with Navy 900 headings and Gold 500 CTAs.
- Cream 50 sections to break up long pages.

## 4. Typography

- **Headings:** Poppins, weights 600 and 700. Use tracking slightly tight on display sizes.
- **Body:** Inter, weight 400 to 500.
- **Numerals and price tags:** Poppins 600 for emphasis.

Scale:
- Display: 56 px / 64 px line height
- H1: 40 px / 48 px
- H2: 32 px / 40 px
- H3: 24 px / 32 px
- Body large: 18 px / 28 px
- Body: 16 px / 26 px
- Caption: 13 px / 20 px

## 5. Voice and Tone

Speak plainly. Keep sentences short. Avoid jargon and inflated marketing language. Sound like a real person who is happy to help.

Do:
- "Book the car you need in a few minutes."
- "We deliver to the airport. Tell us your flight number and we will meet you outside arrivals."
- "Prices are all in. No surprise fees at pickup."

Avoid:
- Heavy adjectives, exclamation points, and stacked superlatives.
- Em dashes in body copy. Use commas, periods, or parentheses instead.
- AI sounding phrases like "elevate your journey" or "unparalleled experience".

## 6. Imagery

- Clean product shots of vehicles on solid or softly graded backgrounds.
- Real travel photography from the island when used in heroes and feature sections.
- Slight cool grade on dark imagery to harmonize with navy.
- Round soft shadows under vehicles. Keep cars centered and well lit.

## 7. UI Patterns

- **Buttons:**
  - Primary: Gold 500 background, Navy 900 text, 12 px radius.
  - Secondary: Navy 900 background, white text.
  - Ghost: Transparent with a 1 px Navy 900 border.
- **Cards:** White surface, soft shadow, 16 px radius, 24 px padding.
- **Sections:** 96 px vertical padding on desktop, 64 px on mobile.
- **Icons:** Lucide style, 1.5 px stroke, Navy 900.

## 9. Site Content Map

The website mirrors the structure of a modern rental site, adapted for All in 1.

1. **Top bar** with contact, hours, language switch.
2. **Navbar** with logo, links, and a Book Now button.
3. **Hero** with headline, supporting line, primary CTA, and a quick booking form (pickup location, pickup date, return date).
4. **Why Choose Us** with four value props (Best Prices, Free Airport Delivery, 24/7 Support, Insurance Included).
5. **Fleet** showcasing categories: Economy, Comfort, SUV, plus a few featured cars with daily rate, seats, transmission, and a Reserve button.
6. **How It Works** in three steps (Choose, Book, Drive).
7. **Services** including airport pickup, long term rentals, and corporate accounts.
8. **About** short paragraph and highlights.
9. **Testimonials** three short quotes from renters.
10. **FAQ** five common questions and answers.
11. **Contact** with form and direct WhatsApp link.
12. **Footer** with navigation, contact info, social, hours, and legal links.

## 10. Booking Form Fields

- Pickup location (select)
- Pickup date and time
- Return date and time
- Vehicle class (optional)
- Customer name, email, phone
- Notes

Submissions should default to a WhatsApp deep link with a prefilled message until a backend is connected.

## 11. SEO and Metadata

- Title: "All in 1 Car Rentals | Reliable Car Rentals on Curaçao"
- Description: "Book a clean, well kept rental car with All in 1. Daily, weekly, and long term rates with free airport delivery."
- Primary keywords: car rental Curaçao, rent a car Curaçao, airport car rental Curaçao, monthly car rental Curaçao.

## 12. Accessibility

- Maintain contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 for body text.
- Provide focus rings on all interactive elements.
- All images include descriptive alt text.
- Forms have associated labels and error messaging.

## 13. Don't List

- No em dashes in copy.
- No stock images with obvious watermarks.
- No bright reds or greens that fight with the navy and gold.
- No generic AI sounding phrases.
- No more than two type sizes in a single card.
