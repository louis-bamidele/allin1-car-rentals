## Long version (email)

Subject: A few questions so I can finish the FAQ on the new website

Hi [Owner's name],

I am close to wrapping up the new All in 1 Car Rentals website. The FAQ section is on the home page and is one of the parts visitors read the most, so I want every answer to reflect how you actually run things. Right now I have placeholders so the layout looks right, but I would like to replace them with real information before we go live.

Could you reply to the questions below? You can answer in short bullets and we can refine the wording together later.

### Renting requirements
1. What is the minimum age to rent? Is there an extra fee for drivers under 25?
2. Do you require an international driver license, or is a regular license fine?
3. What documents must the renter bring at pickup? (license, passport or ID, credit card)

### Pricing and payment
4. What is the daily rate range right now for each category (Economy, Comfort, SUV)?
5. What is the security deposit and how is it held? (Credit card pre auth, cash, other)
6. Which payment methods do you accept? (Visa, Mastercard, cash, bank transfer)
7. Are taxes and fees already included in the price shown, or added at pickup?
8. Do you offer weekly and monthly discounts? Starting from how many days?

### Insurance and coverage
9. What insurance is included with every rental?
10. What is the deductible if there is damage?
11. Do you offer a top up insurance? How much?
12. What is not covered? (Tires, glass, underbody, anything else)

### Pickup, delivery, hours
13. Is airport delivery free at Hato Airport? What about hotel delivery on the island?
14. Are there areas where you do not deliver?
15. What are your operating hours, and do you charge for after hours pickup or drop off?

### Cancellation and changes
16. What is your cancellation policy? Free cancellation up to how many hours before pickup?
17. What happens if a renter does not show up?
18. Can renters extend their booking during the trip, and how?

### During the rental
19. Is there a mileage limit, or unlimited kilometers?
20. What is the fuel policy? Pick up full and return full, or pre paid fuel?
21. What should renters do if they get a flat tire, a breakdown, or are in an accident? Who do they call and is there a phone number you want printed on the dashboard card?
22. Do you offer extras like a child seat, booster seat, GPS, or extra driver? If yes, what are the rates?

### Anything else
23. Are there any rules you want to make very clear on the website? (No smoking, no off road, no taking the car off the island, etc.)
24. Anything you wish renters asked before booking but they often forget?

Once you reply, I will rewrite the FAQ in the website voice and send you a preview to approve. If there is anything you are not sure about yet, just leave it blank and I will keep that question off the site until we have the answer.

Thanks a lot.

[Your name]
